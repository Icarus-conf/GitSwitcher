# Code of conduct

GitSwitcher follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- **Be kind and assume good faith.** People contribute in their spare time,
  in their second or third language, at every level of experience.
- **Disagree about the work, not the person.** Explain why, and be open to
  being wrong.
- **No harassment.** No insults, personal attacks, unwelcome sexual
  attention, or publishing anyone's private information.
- **Help newcomers.** A first question is never a stupid one.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else you
represent the project.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately to the maintainer through
[a private security report](https://github.com/Icarus-conf/GitSwitcher/security/advisories/new)
(it reaches the maintainer only), or by contacting the maintainer through
their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may
remove comments, close threads, or block people who don't follow this code.
